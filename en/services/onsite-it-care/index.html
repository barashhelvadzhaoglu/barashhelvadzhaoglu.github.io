The solution: I set up automatic, encrypted data backup from your NAS directly to AWS Cloud (Frankfurt).

  • Automatic: Daily backup without manual intervention
  • Encrypted: Your data is encrypted before transfer — only you have the key
  • Your AWS account: I set everything up in your name — the data and account belong to you
  • GDPR-compliant: Servers in Frankfurt (EU), no data sharing with third parties
  • Affordable: 100 GB in AWS S3 costs approximately €2-3 per month

For medical practices & law firms: I prepare a Data Processing Agreement (AVV) — legally clean and GDPR-compliant.

🔋 UPS – Uninterruptible Power Supply#

The problem: A power outage or voltage spike can permanently damage your server, NAS or network devices and cause data loss — often without warning.

The solution: A UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) protects your devices from power outages and voltage surges.

How it works:

Power goes out
      ↓
UPS kicks in immediately (battery)
      ↓
NAS detects the outage automatically
      ↓
Safe shutdown within 2 minutes
      ↓
No data loss, no damage
  • Recommendation: APC or Eaton Line-Interactive UPS
  • NAS integration: Synology NAS detects UPS automatically via USB and shuts down safely
  • Annual battery check: I check the battery condition once a year
