Cisco Collaboration Support & Managed Services#

Your Cisco Collaboration infrastructure runs your business communication — IP phones, video conferencing, Jabber, Webex. When it breaks, work stops. When it’s poorly managed, it becomes a hidden cost center.

With 10+ years of hands-on experience across dozens of Cisco Collaboration deployments — banking, manufacturing, aviation, petrochemical — I provide remote managed services, support, and new installations for organizations that need a specialist without the cost of a full-time engineer.

💬 Free technical consultation: WhatsApp or Email

What I Support#

📞 Cisco Unified Communications Manager (CUCM)#

Full lifecycle management of your CallManager infrastructure:

  • Day-to-day administration — users, extensions, dial plans, hunt groups
  • Troubleshooting call quality issues, registration failures, SIP trunk problems
  • CUCM version upgrades (8.x → 14.x) with zero service interruption
  • License management and compliance monitoring
  • Backup configuration and disaster recovery planning

💬 Cisco Jabber & Webex#

  • Jabber deployment and configuration for desktop and mobile
  • Expressway Edge/Core setup for VPN-less external access
  • Webex App integration with on-premise CUCM (Hybrid deployment)
  • MRA (Mobile and Remote Access) troubleshooting
  • User onboarding and training support

📹 Video Conferencing#

  • Cisco Room systems (Webex Room Kit, Board series) deployment and management
  • Multipoint conferencing setup
  • Integration with Microsoft Teams and Zoom via interoperability gateways
  • Scheduled maintenance and firmware updates

🔧 Supporting Infrastructure#

  • Unity Connection (CUC): Voicemail, auto-attendant, IVR configuration
  • IM & Presence: Status, federation, persistent chat
  • SIP Trunk management: Provider integration, codec negotiation, failover
  • Voice gateways: MGCP, H.323, SIP gateway configuration
