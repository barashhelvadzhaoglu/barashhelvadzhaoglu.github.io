Enterprise WiFi & Network Design#

Poor wireless coverage, network bottlenecks, and ad-hoc infrastructure decisions accumulate into real business problems — dropped video calls, slow file transfers, security gaps, and projects that fail at go-live.

With 11+ years of enterprise network and wireless experience — designing and deploying Cisco and Aruba environments across hotels, factories, campuses, warehouses, and corporate offices — I provide network design, wireless planning, and infrastructure project delivery for organizations that need it done right.

💬 Free technical consultation: WhatsApp or Email

What I Do#

📡 Enterprise Wireless (WLAN) Design#

  • RF planning and coverage design — heat mapping, capacity planning, AP placement
  • Cisco and Aruba wireless architecture (controller-based and cloud-managed)
  • High-density environments: hotels, conference centers, warehouses, hospitals
  • Guest and staff network segmentation (VLAN, SSID isolation)
  • Roaming optimization and seamless mobility
  • Ekahau site survey for predictive and post-deployment validation

🏗️ Network Architecture & LAN/WAN Design#

  • Campus and branch network design (Core / Distribution / Access)
  • VLAN design and segmentation strategy
  • Routing protocol selection and configuration (OSPF, BGP, EIGRP)
  • SD-WAN design and implementation
  • High availability and redundancy planning

🏢 Office Build & Relocation Projects#

  • New office network infrastructure from scratch
  • Office relocation — network cutover planning and execution
  • Structured cabling coordination
  • Integration with existing corporate infrastructure

☁️ Cloud-Managed Networking#

  • Cisco Meraki — full-stack cloud management (MX/MS/MR)
  • Aruba Central — cloud management for campus and branch
  • Centralized monitoring, alerting, and lifecycle management

  1. Discovery — I review your current infrastructure, understand requirements, identify gaps
  2. Design — High-Level Design (HLD) and Low-Level Design (LLD) with bill of materials
  3. Delivery — Remote configuration + on-site coordination as needed
  4. Handover — Full documentation, training for your team

Typical project investment: €800 – €8,000 depending on scope and site count.
