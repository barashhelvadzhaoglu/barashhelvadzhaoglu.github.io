Senior Network Security Engineer & Architect#

I am a dynamic and results-driven Senior Network Security Engineer with more than 11 years of experience designing, implementing, and securing enterprise networks across banking, manufacturing, and consulting industries.

To explore selected projects I have delivered across banking, manufacturing, hospitality, and aviation — visit my Project Portfolio.

A professional reference letter from senior management is available upon request.

Professional Expertise#

I specialize in multi-vendor environments and modern network paradigms:

  • Vendors: Cisco, Palo Alto, Fortinet, Check Point, Aruba, F5.
  • Focus Areas: Zero Trust Architecture, Network Automation, and Data Center Modernization (VXLAN/BGP-EVPN, NDFC, SD-Access).
  • Automation & IaC: Enhancing operational efficiency using Python, Bash, Ansible, and Terraform.

Leadership & Methodology#

Beyond technical implementation, I have a proven track record as a Network Security Team Lead. I am experienced in:

  • Managing cross-functional and self-organizing engineering teams.
  • ITSM & Change Management within Agile/Scrum frameworks.
  • Improving SLA performance through DevSecOps collaboration.

“Building secure, automated and scalable network infrastructures — one architecture at a time.”
