Firewall Management & Security Audit Services#

Your firewall is your first line of defense — but only if it’s properly configured and actively maintained. Bloated rule sets, outdated firmware, misconfigured VPN tunnels, and unmonitored open ports are silent risks that accumulate over time.

With 11+ years of enterprise firewall experience across banking, manufacturing, and critical infrastructure — managing 100+ physical and virtual firewalls simultaneously — I provide remote firewall management, optimization, and security audits for organizations that need a specialist without the overhead of a full-time engineer.

💬 Free technical consultation: WhatsApp or Email

What I Do#

🔥 Firewall Rule Management#

  • Review and cleanup of existing rule sets — removing redundant, shadowed, or overly permissive rules
  • Policy optimization for performance and security
  • Change management and documentation of every modification
  • Compliance reporting (ISO 27001, PCI DSS, NIS2)

🔒 VPN Setup & Optimization#

  • IPsec site-to-site VPN — new setup, troubleshooting, failover configuration
  • SSL-VPN — remote access for users (Fortinet FortiClient, Palo Alto GlobalProtect, Cisco AnyConnect)
  • VPN tunnel monitoring and alerting
  • Multi-vendor VPN interoperability

🛡️ Security Audits#

  • Open port analysis and unnecessary service exposure
  • Firewall policy review against best-practice frameworks (OWASP, CIS)
  • Log analysis for anomalous traffic patterns
  • Actionable remediation report with prioritized findings

⚙️ Firmware & Patch Management#

  • Scheduled firmware updates with rollback planning
  • Vulnerability assessment for known CVEs in your firewall platform
  • Zero-downtime update execution for HA environments
