Web Shield – Professional Website Protection & Management#

Your website is often the first impression potential customers have of your business. A crash, a cyberattack, or an expired SSL certificate can cost you customers and trust — often without you even noticing.

Web Shield is a monthly all-inclusive protection plan for your business website: security, maintenance, backups, and monitoring — all from one person, so you never have to worry about it.

💬 Free website analysis: Write on WhatsApp — I’ll check your website for vulnerabilities at no cost.

What’s Included?#

🔒 SSL Certificate – Always Valid, Never Expired#

An expired SSL certificate shows your visitors the warning “This site is not secure” — and they leave immediately.

  • Automatic setup and renewal
  • No manual action required
  • Your site is always accessible with the green padlock

🛡️ WAF – Web Application Firewall#

A WAF protects your website from the most common attacks: SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), bot attacks, and more.

  • OWASP security ruleset activation
  • Automatic blocking of suspicious requests
  • 24/7 protection against hacking attempts

⚡ DDoS Protection – Your Website Stays Online#

DDoS attacks flood your website with requests until it crashes. With our protection, these attacks are automatically repelled — before they cause damage.

  • Unlimited DDoS protection
  • Automatic detection and mitigation
  • No downtime from attacks

🌐 Free Hosting (for eligible websites)#

Are you still paying monthly for hosting at Ionos, Strato, or another provider?

For many simple business websites, we can migrate hosting entirely to Cloudflare Pages — free, faster, and more secure than traditional hosting.

  • No more monthly hosting costs
  • Global CDN — your site loads fast worldwide
  • Automatic deployments when changes are made

Note: Suitable for static websites and simple business pages. Complex WordPress or e-commerce systems stay with existing hosting — but still receive WAF and DDoS protection.

🔑 Your Credentials – Always Under Your Control#

This is especially important to us: All accounts belong to you.

For each client we set up their own account:

  • Cloudflare account in your name → DNS, WAF, SSL, Hosting
  • GitHub account in your name → Your website files
  • Password Manager (Bitwarden) → All credentials stored securely

I only receive an administrator access — so I can handle the management. You retain full control at all times. Should you wish to end the service, everything belongs to you. No lock-in, no hidden dependencies.

Frequently Asked Questions#

Do I need to change my website or hosting provider? No. We only change the DNS settings of your domain — your website stays where it is. The entire process takes less than an hour and is imperceptible to your visitors.

What happens if I cancel the service? All accounts (Cloudflare, GitHub) belong to you. You can keep everything or hand it over to another provider. No lock-in.

My website was created by an agency — is that a problem? No. We work with any existing website, regardless of who created it.

How quickly can I get started? After a short initial consultation, your protection is usually active within 24 hours.

I have WordPress — does this work too? Yes. WAF and DDoS protection work with any website. WordPress updates and backups are included in the Business package.
