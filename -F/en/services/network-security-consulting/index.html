Core Competencies & Solutions#

1. Next-Generation Firewall (NGFW) Management#

Your firewall is the first line of defense. I provide not just installation, but optimization based on best-practice standards:

  • Palo Alto & Fortinet Migration: Seamless migration from legacy systems to modern NGFW platforms with Zero Downtime.
  • Policy Optimization: Cleaning up complex rulebases to boost system performance and security.
  • SSL Inspection: Gaining visibility into hidden threats within encrypted traffic.

2. Zero Trust & Secure Access Architecture#

Modernizing your network based on the principle of “Never trust, always verify”:

  • Micro-Segmentation: Limiting lateral movement within the network to prevent the spread of security breaches.
  • Secure Remote Access (VPN/ZTNA): Implementing Palo Alto GlobalProtect or Fortinet ZTNA for secure mobile work.
  • Identity Management (Cisco ISE / Aruba ClearPass): Automated verification of identity and security posture for every device on the network.

3. Data Center & Campus Network Design#

High-speed infrastructures for modern requirements:

  • Cisco NDFC & VXLAN: Scalable and flexible fabric architectures for modern data centers.
  • High Availability (HA): Redundant designs to ensure continuous operation during hardware or software failures.
  • SD-WAN Implementation: Cost-effective and intelligent wide-area network management for global sites.

4. Network Automation (NetDevOps)#

Modern approaches that eliminate manual errors and increase speed:

  • Python-based Automation: Executing policy changes or inventory audits on hundreds of devices simultaneously.
  • Configuration Backup & Monitoring: Comprehensive logging of every change in your infrastructure for audits and compliance.
