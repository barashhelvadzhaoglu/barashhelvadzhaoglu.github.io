Your Guest WiFi as a Marketing Tool#

Every guest who logs into your WiFi leaves their email address or phone number — automatically and GDPR-compliant. No manual work. No extra costs for your business.

How It Works#

1. Guest connects to your WiFi A branded splash page with your logo opens automatically.

2. Guest enters email or phone number One click on “Accept” — and they are online. You now have their contact details.

3. Build your customer list All data is securely stored in your dashboard. Weekly email reports included.

4. Send targeted offers Use the collected contacts for newsletters, promotions, or loyalty discounts.

Frequently Asked Questions#

Do I need new hardware? Usually not. If your existing device is compatible, we only add a small add-on device (starting at €79).

Is it GDPR compliant? Yes. Guests actively consent to data collection. All data is stored encrypted on German servers.

How much customer data can I collect? An average cafe collects between 200–500 new contacts per month.

Can I use the data for email marketing? Yes, you can export contacts directly or we can set up automated campaigns for you.
